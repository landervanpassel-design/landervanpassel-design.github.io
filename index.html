Stochastic Reconstruction Engine — Power Grid Resilience

2025 – 2026 · Live demo

Interactive browser demo of the Van Passel CDI framework on the IEEE 9-bus system: Simulation, Benchmark, and 2003 Event tabs with CSV export. In a six-method simulation benchmark on shared stochastic noise paths, CDI is the only method with an a-priori guarantee: a certified closed-form bound on mean recovery time (E[τ] ≤ 3.53 s; 0.19 s observed at defaults). On a stylised August 14 2003 Northeast Blackout replay (4 scenarios × 30 seeded paths), CDI recovers on all simulated paths vs 0% uncontrolled; combined CDI+UFLS blocks all four cascade stages on all paths under the stated model assumptions.

Protected Desire Equilibrium (PDE)

2025 – 2026 · Multi-agent Active Inference

Conceptual multi-agent simulation framework proposing PDE as a generalised Active Inference objective, with a bridging argument that situates PDE relative to Nash equilibrium and Lyapunov stability within a Unified Stability Ontology.
